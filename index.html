Capture Aurora Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short: your photos and videos stay on your iPhone. Capture Aurora has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it never uploads anything you record.

Who we are

Capture Aurora is made by Sebastiaan Vryens ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email vryens.sebastiaan@gmail.com.

Your photos and videos

Everything you capture is processed on your device and stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. We never receive, upload or see your photos or videos. They are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Copies you have saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to record videos and take photos. The camera image is processed on your device only.
  • Photo library: to save your captures to Photos when you choose (or automatically, if you turn that on), and to import the photos and videos you pick. The app only uses the items you choose.
  • Microphone: not used. Capture Aurora records silent video.

Settings

Your settings, such as whether captures are saved to Photos automatically, are stored on your device only.

App updates

To deliver bug fixes quickly, the app checks for small updates through Expo, a service of 650 Industries, Inc. When it checks, it sends:

  • a random identifier created for this installation of the app, which isn't linked to your name, account or any other app;
  • the app's version and the platform it runs on (iOS).

Like any internet request, this check also reveals your device's IP address to Expo's servers. This information is used only to deliver updates and to count how many installations are active. It is not used for advertising or tracking, and it is not combined with any other data about you. Expo's handling of this data is described in Expo's privacy policy.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports and anonymous usage statistics with us. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't require an account.

Children

Capture Aurora is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union or another region with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we don't hold any data that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to look up. If you have a question or request, email us at the address above. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date at the top.
