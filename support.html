Frequently asked questions

Why does the picture look bright and choppy?

Capture Aurora is made for the night. Each frame stays exposed much longer than in normal video, so a faint aurora shows up. In daylight or a bright room, that makes the picture overexposed, and the longer frames mean fewer frames per second. Outside on a dark night, it looks right.

How do I change shutter, ISO, focus or white balance?

Tap anywhere on the camera screen to show the settings, then tap Shutter, ISO, Focus or WB. Tap the screen again to hide them. A longer shutter and a higher ISO make the picture brighter. The sky is usually sharpest with focus near 0.80.

Where are my videos and photos saved?

Inside the app, until you save them to Photos. To save them automatically, show the settings, tap Photos and turn on Save to Photos. To save some yourself, open the gallery, tap Select, choose the items and tap Save to Photos.

Important: if you delete the app, anything you haven't saved to Photos is deleted with it.

How do I make a sped-up or slowed-down copy?

Open a video in the gallery and tap Edit. Pick a speed and tap Save as copy. The new clip appears next to the original, which stays as it is.

Can I add videos or photos I took with another app?

Yes. Open the gallery and tap Import to choose them from your photo library.

Tips for the best results

  • Use a tripod or rest your iPhone on something steady. Long exposures blur when the phone moves.
  • Find a spot away from street lights.
  • Give the camera as much dark sky as you can.
